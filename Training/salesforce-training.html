FAQ

Everything You Need to Know

Who is this training designed for? ▾
This program is ideal for freshers (any graduation), career switchers from IT/non-IT backgrounds, and working professionals looking to upskill into Salesforce Admin, Developer, or Architect roles. No prior Salesforce experience is required.
Is this training online or offline? ▾
We offer both live online and in-person classroom training at our centers in Hyderabad and Bengaluru. All live sessions are recorded and available for 6 months so you never miss a class.
How long does the course take and what are the batch timings? ▾
The standard batch runs for 3 months (weekday or weekend). A fast-track batch is available in 6 weeks for experienced IT professionals. Weekday batches run Mon–Fri (7–9am or 7–9pm IST), and weekend batches run Sat–Sun (10am–1pm or 2–5pm IST).
What is your placement guarantee? ▾
We offer placement support — not just assistance. This includes résumé building, mock technical and HR interviews, direct referrals to our 200+ hiring partner companies, and continued job support until you're placed. 96% of our students get placed within 60 days of course completion.
Do I get a Salesforce Developer Org to practice on? ▾
Yes! Every student gets a dedicated Salesforce Developer Org from Day 1, configured for all projects. We also provide access to tools like Workbench, VS Code with Salesforce extensions, and a pre-built CI/CD pipeline for the developer track.
Are the certification exam fees included? ▾
The course fee does not include Salesforce certification exam vouchers (each exam is ~$200 USD), but we have a partnership that gives our students a 40% discount on exam vouchers. We also provide an exam prep bundle — practice tests, study guides, and a pre-exam coaching session.
What is the course fee and are there EMI options? ▾
We offer transparent, competitive pricing with 0% EMI options via our banking partners. Book a free demo class first — our counsellors will walk you through a personalised fee structure based on your track and batch preference. No hidden fees, ever.
